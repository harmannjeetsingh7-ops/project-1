About the Selectric

A closer look at the design, engineering, and features that made the Selectric a landmark office machine.

The Selectric Mechanism

Unlike traditional typewriters, the Selectric replaced typebars with a spherical element that rotates and tilts to print letters. This eliminated jamming and ensured cleaner alignment, even during fast typing.

Office Advantages

  • Consistent, sharp character printing
  • Reduced mechanical wear over time
  • Interchangeable typeballs for quick font and language changes
  • Lower operational noise in busy offices

Specifications

Typing Speed: Up to 12 characters per second

Pitch Options: Pica (10) and Elite (12)

Durability: Built for high-volume professional use

Comfortable Keyboard

Key travel and tension were engineered to reduce fatigue during long typing sessions.

Consistent Line Spacing

The carriage system provides uniform line spacing to maintain a clean and structured page.

Modular Construction

Many components can be serviced independently, shortening downtime during maintenance.
